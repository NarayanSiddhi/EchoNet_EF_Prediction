Comment: Pediatric EF estimation is a clinically realistic and important problem, and the paper's strongest contribution is not the small architectural improvement but the observation that performance differences across age groups substantially exceed the difference between architectures. However, the paper sometimes interprets these differences too causally. The statement that the infant performance gap “follows from the physiology of infancy” is plausible, but the proposed mechanisms—higher heart rate yielding fewer cardiac cycles and smaller chambers occupying fewer pixels—are not directly tested.
